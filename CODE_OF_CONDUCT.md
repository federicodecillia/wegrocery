# Code of Conduct

WeGrocery adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and accept
constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people using the
app. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing
others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer,
[Federico De Cillia](https://github.com/federicodecillia), privately by email
at [federico.decillia@gmail.com](mailto:federico.decillia@gmail.com). Reports
are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, as
described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
